## 1. Findings (Pre-Writing Report)

1. **AudioListener Discovery & Caching:**
   - Cached lazily via `FindFirstObjectByType<AudioListener>()` in `Start()`.
   - If missing or switched (e.g. following camera swaps), safely re-queried inside the periodic `checkInterval` check, never every frame in `Update()`.
2. **Occluder Layer Mask & Open Doors:**
   - Serialized `LayerMask occluderMask` (defaults to solid obstacles, walls, doors).
   - Doors that open disable or swing their physical collider out of line of sight (standard World lane door behavior). When open, `Physics.Linecast` does not intersect the door collider, leaving the audio unoccluded.
3. **Performance Optimization (Zero GC & Low Cost):**
   - Single non-allocating `Physics.Linecast` per source executed strictly on a timer (`occlusionCheckInterval = 0.15s`), NOT every frame.
   - Distance guard: sources beyond `occlusionMaxDistance` (default 25m) skip raycasts entirely.
   - Random timer offset (`Random.Range(0, checkInterval)`) applied at `Start()` staggers checks across sources so they never linecast on the exact same frame.
4. **Smooth Interpolation & Target Values:**
   - Per-frame `Mathf.Lerp` using `Time.deltaTime * occlusionSmoothing` (default smoothing = 8.0).
   - **Target Values:**
     - Open cutoff: `22000 Hz` (full audible bandwidth).
     - Occluded cutoff: `1200 Hz` (muffled, absorbs high frequencies).
     - Occluded volume: `0.6` (attenuated by 40%).
   - `AudioLowPassFilter.enabled` is toggled off when the filter reaches open state (`cutoff >= 21900 Hz` and unoccluded) to completely eliminate DSP overhead when not needed.
5. **InteractTuning Fields:**
   - Added the following 6 fields to `InteractTuning.cs`:
     - `occlusionCheckInterval = 0.15f`
     - `occlusionMaxDistance = 25.0f`
     - `occlusionOpenCutoff = 22000f`
     - `occlusionCutoff = 1200f`
     - `occlusionVolume = 0.6f`
     - `occlusionSmoothing = 8.0f`
