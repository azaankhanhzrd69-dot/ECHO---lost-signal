## Architecture & Findings
1. **Minigame Start Mechanism**:
   - `EchoEvents.HideStarted` and `EchoEvents.StartHide(Vector3 spot)` **already exist** in `EchoEvents.cs` (lines 33, 83). No contract addition was needed. `HideMinigame` subscribes directly to `EchoEvents.HideStarted`.
2. **Player Controls & Cursor Lock**:
   - `EchoEvents.LockInput()` locks look and movement via `EchoPlayerMotor`.
   - Cursor is set to `CursorLockMode.Locked` and `Cursor.visible = false` so mouse movements apply physical deflection without mouse cursor escaping.
3. **GameTuning Integration**:
   - `GameTuning` already contains:
     - `bangFillPerSecond` (0.35f)
     - `bangDecayPerSecond` (0.5f)
   - Other tunables (`minigameDuration`, `ringRadius`, `shakeStrength`, `shakeFrequency`, `mouseSensitivity`) are surfaced as Inspector fields on `HideMinigame` with sensible defaults.
