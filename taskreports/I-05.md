1. **Exact Message and Usable Condition:**
   - **Message:** `"SOS"` (confirmed from `SecretNotePickup.cs`: `FREQ: 142.85 MHz\nTRANSMIT: SOS`).
   - **Storyboard quote:** `STORYBOARD.md` line 65–67:
     > *"Note: A secret note containing a hidden message and a radio frequency can be found in one of the dead astronaut's pockets. He can take this note to the radio room, where he will have to convert the message into Morse and send it through the radio. No morse translater will be provided. Once this is done, the secret sequence will trigger after the good ending."*

   - **No noise:** The storyboard mentions power returning alerts the monster (`"After the energy cores are added and the power comes back, the monster realises where you are and chases after you"`). The radio transmission itself does not emit noise (`EchoEvents.MakeNoise` is NOT called).

4. **Player Lock and Cursor Management:**
   - Handled directly via `Cursor.lockState` and `Cursor.visible`.
   - Exposed `UnityEvent OnOpened` and `UnityEvent OnClosed` for the human / session to wire to Core input lock (`EchoEvents.LockInput()` / `EchoEvents.UnlockInput()`).
5. **Input System:**
   - Package: `"com.unity.inputsystem": "1.20.1"`.
   - Uses `UnityEngine.InputSystem.Keyboard.current` for `escapeKey` (close), `enterKey` (send), `backspaceKey` (delete), `periodKey` (dot), `minusKey` (dash), `spaceKey` (gap).
6. **InteractTuning Fields:**
