## Findings & Spec Gaps
1. **Target Core Count**: Existing `EchoGame.cs` checks for `coresFound < 3` (3 cores required). However, neither `Progress` nor `GameTuning` currently exposes a `requiredCores` constant or tunable.
2. **Double Collection Prevention**: Handled via `bool isCollected` inside `PickupBase.Interact()`, immediately returning early and deactivating the GameObject (or disabling its Collider & Renderers).
3. **CoreHunt -> PowerOn transition**: The contract does not define an automated trigger or subscriber for transitioning `Progress.Current` to `Beat.PowerOn` upon finding all cores; this belongs to reactor insertion/Core sequence logic (Beat 11).
