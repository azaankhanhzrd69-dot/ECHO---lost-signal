## 1. Findings (Pre-Writing Report)

1. **Ending Rules & Plan:**
   - Pure C# `EndingRules.Evaluate(bool morseSent)` returns `EndingPlan { HasGood = true, HasSecret = morseSent, Order = GoodThenSecret }`.
   - **Storyboard quote (`STORYBOARD.md` line 67):**
     > *"Once this is done, the secret sequence will trigger after the good ending."*
   - Good Ending is always presented; Secret Ending triggers only if `Progress.MorseSent` is true.
2. **Reaching the Escape Pod:**
   - Implemented `PodReachedTrigger` with a trigger collider at the escape pod hatch.
   - Detects the player via tag `"Player"` or `CharacterController`.
   - Advances beat to `Beat.Airlock` strictly when `Progress.Current == Beat.Chase`.
3. **Airlock Sequence & World Staging:**
   - On `Beat.Airlock`: locks input, puts creature in scripted mode (`monster.EnterScripted()`), invokes `OnAirlockBegin`.
   - Waits for World cutscene to invoke `AirlockFinished()`, guarded by safety timeout `tuning.airlockTimeoutSeconds`.
   - Advances to `Beat.Ending`.
4. **Ending Flow & Sequencing:**
   - On `Beat.Ending`: invokes `OnEndingBegin`, evaluates plan.
   - Invokes `OnGoodEnding`, waits `goodEndingSeconds` or `GoodEndingFinished()`.
   - If `plan.HasSecret`: invokes `OnSecretEnding`, waits `secretEndingSeconds` or `SecretEndingFinished()`.
   - Invokes `OnEndingFinished`.
5. **Ownership of Ending Screens:**
   - I-09's `ScreenManager` already listens to `EchoEvents.BeatChanged(Beat.Ending)` and evaluates `Progress.MorseSent`.
   - `EndingController` owns the Core staging sequence and triggers UnityEvents `OnGoodEnding` and `OnSecretEnding`. Either `ScreenManager` displays the narrative cards automatically on `Beat.Ending`, or can be wired via `EndingController`'s events.
6. **Death Immunity in Airlock/Ending:**
   - `DeathRespawnController` ignores death when `Current` is `Airlock` or `Ending`.
   - `monster.EnterScripted()` in Airlock halts monster attacks.
   - Touching the pod trigger in the same frame as a monster hit immediately sets `Beat.Airlock`, rendering the player immune.
7. **GameTuning Fields:**
   - Added:
     - `airlockTimeoutSeconds = 8.0f`
     - `goodEndingSeconds = 8.0f`
     - `secretEndingSeconds = 8.0f`
