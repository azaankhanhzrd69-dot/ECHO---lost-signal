## 1. Coordinate System & Overall Scale

- **Origin $(0, 0, 0)$:** Centre of the main deck. Floor is at $Y = 0.0\text{ m}$.
- **Axes:**
  - $+X$: East
  - $-X$: West
  - $+Z$: North
  - $-Z$: South
  - $+Y$: Up
- **Hull Extents:**
  - **Main Deck Hull:** $X \in [-24.0, +24.0]$, $Z \in [-24.0, +24.0]$ ($48\text{ m} \times 48\text{ m}$)
  - **Docking Bay:** $X \in [-4.0, +8.0]$, $Z \in [-36.0, -24.0]$ ($12\text{ m} \times 12\text{ m}$)
  - **Wall Thickness:** $0.3\text{ m}$
  - **Ceiling Height:** $3.5\text{ m}$ standard ($5.0\text{ m}$ in Reactor; open sky in Docking Bay)
  - **Door Openings:** $2.4\text{ m}$ wide $\times 3.0\text{ m}$ high
  - **Hallway Lanes:** Minimum $3.0\text{ m}$ width (exceeds $2.5\text{ m}$ minimum constraint)
