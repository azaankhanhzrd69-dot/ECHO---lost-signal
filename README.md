# GAMEPLAY: 

```
(Cutscene)
John: Mission Control, Star Command 141 is on board the StarGazer. I repeat, I have boarded the StarGazer!

Mission Control: Roger that Star 141. Remember. Your task is to relay the information about Task Force 11, and retrieve the reports of the StarGazer's latest assignment.

John: "Roger that, Sir. Star Command 141 is Oscar Mike".

John: (Damn this place is cold)

(End)

The first sequence requires the player to board off their shuttle, walk into the decompression chamber of the StarGazer and look for a paper containing the keypad entry code and a map of the spaceship.
This is found in the vent. The player opens the door and walks into the dark spaceship

As the player makes their way to the MedBay, they see the mangled body of an astronaut from Task Force 11, which has been cut open and it's blood has splayed everywhere. 

(thinking)
John: "Dear God"
John: "I must get out of here"

(Cutscene)
As John reaches for the MedBay exit, he notices a dark red creature with no eyes devouring the bodies of one of the three dead task force members. 
He gasps in fear, and notices the creature dash towards him.
He escapes the medbay and locks the door, but the creature is trying to break his way out.
(end)

Here, the player is given the choice to hide in three places: Under the chair, in a locker or under a desk.
The closet is the only right choice, the other two trigger death cutscenes as he is found.
When he enters the closet, a minigame plays where the player will have to keep the mouse pointer at the center as the monster bangs the door and tries to pry his way in. 

After the monster leaves, the player will have to walk to his shuttle without alerting the monster. Every step where he walks or runs alerts it more.
Basically echolocation monster rules.  I don't have to elaborate on this. 

(Cutscene)
Once he reaches his space ship, he realises that his engine has been crushed to bits and pieces and the only way out is to power an escape pod.
(end)

The player walks through the dark, taking special care as to not alert the creature.
Once he reaches the reactor room to enable the power, he notices that the power cores are missing. 
This is when he sees a map of the spaceship with the location of the spare power cores in case the originals get lost.

Special gameplay feature: If the monster approaches close, the player can hide in the nearest closet and they can play the same minigame.
This will happen up to three times after the initial game, and the fourth time the monster will take him out and eat him

After he finds the cores and gets to the reacter room, he puts them back on. 

Note: A secret note containing a hidden message and a radio frequency can be found in one of the dead astronaut's pockets.
He can take this note to the radio room, where he will have to convert the message into Morse and send it through the radio. 
No morse translater will be provided. Once this is done, the secret sequence will trigger after the good ending. 

After the energy cores are added and the power comes back, the monster realises where you are and chases after you.
Make a run for the spaceship.
```
